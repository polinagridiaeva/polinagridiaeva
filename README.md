## Polina Gridiaeva

I work on machine learning for physics data: reconstructing physical quantities from detector signals, working with simulations and building data pipelines for experiments. I'm also interested in applying ML to biological and medical data.

MSc student in Physics of Data at the University of Padova<br>
BSc in Applied Mathematics and Physics from Saint Petersburg State University

**Selected projects**

- **Energy reconstruction in heavy-ion collisions**: neural networks that estimate the energy released in Au+Au collisions at NICA from the signal of two small detectors
- **Muon track reconstruction**: a PointNet-style network that predicts the entry and exit points of cosmic muons in a water Cherenkov detector
- **COVID-19 papers with Apache Spark**: distributed analysis of 128,000 research papers on a multi-node cluster
